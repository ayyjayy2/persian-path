Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Persian Learnin is a free app for learning Persian. This page explains what it stores about you, where, and how to delete it.

Without an account

You can use the whole app without an account. Your progress is then kept only on your device, in the app's own storage, and is never sent anywhere.

With an account

If you create an account, your progress syncs between your devices. To do that, the app stores the following in Google Firebase:

  • Your email address, which is your sign-in name.
  • Your learning progress: lessons and activities done, XP, streak, your review schedule, words you got wrong, your settings, which grammar and script pages you've opened, and the text of words you added in My words.

Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication. The app never stores it or sees it after you sign in.

Firebase is run by Google. Its privacy and security terms are at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Kept only on your device

  • Pictures you add to My words.
  • Recordings from speaking practice. They exist only while you're on that exercise so you can play them back. They are never saved or uploaded.

Other services the app loads

Like any website, these services see your IP address when the app loads something from them:

  • Google Fonts, for the Persian font.
  • Wikimedia, for the photos and flags in culture articles.
  • OpenStreetMap, for the maps.

No ads, analytics or tracking

The app shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking tools. Your information is never sold or shared.

Deleting your data

With an account: go to Profile → Delete account. This deletes your account, everything stored with it, and the progress on that device. It can't be undone.

Without an account: delete the app from your home screen, or clear this website's data in your browser.

Children

The app isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
